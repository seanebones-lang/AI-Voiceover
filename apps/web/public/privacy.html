Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 13, 2026

1. Introduction

NextEleven LLC ("NextEleven," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our voice synthesis and cloning platform ("Service").

NextEleven is a self-hosted voice AI platform. When you self-host our software, your data remains on your own infrastructure and is not transmitted to NextEleven servers unless you explicitly use our cloud services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and password when creating an account
  • Voice Data: Audio recordings you upload for voice cloning purposes
  • Text Content: Text you input for text-to-speech conversion
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed through secure third-party payment processors

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information and browser type
  • IP address and general location
  • Usage analytics and feature interactions
  • Error logs and performance data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our Service
  • To process voice synthesis and cloning requests
  • To improve our AI models and service quality
  • To communicate with you about updates and support
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

For self-hosted deployments, all data remains on your infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and monitoring

5. Voice Data Handling

Voice samples you upload for cloning purposes are:

  • Stored securely on your self-hosted infrastructure
  • Used solely for creating your voice clone
  • Never shared with third parties without explicit consent
  • Deletable at any time through your account settings

⚠️ Important: You must have proper consent and legal rights to clone any voice. Unauthorized voice cloning may violate laws and our Terms of Service.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for:

  • Payment processing (Stripe)
  • Analytics (privacy-focused alternatives)
  • Error monitoring

These services have their own privacy policies governing data handling.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent at any time

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

For self-hosted deployments, data remains in your chosen jurisdiction. For cloud services, data may be processed in the United States. We comply with applicable data protection laws including GDPR for EU users.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or through the Service. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
